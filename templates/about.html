OUR MISSION

Making the connection
faster and simpler.

Finding a suitable blood donor can become difficult when every minute matters.

Blood Finder aims to simplify that first connection by allowing users to search for registered donors according to blood group and location.

The platform combines a simple interface, location-based matching and a donor database to make the search process easier.

♥

One connection
can make a difference.

We believe technology can make it easier for people to reach the right person when help is needed.
